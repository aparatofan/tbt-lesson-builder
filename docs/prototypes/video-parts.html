Video parts

Paste the video once. Then fill in each part: its time, its flashcards, its questions.

What changed from the live builder

  1. Questions are pasted inside each part. The one big paste box at the top and the timeline bar are gone.
  2. Each part has three rows, one under another: Time → Flashcards → Questions, each with a tick when it’s done.
  3. Times accept 55 as well as 0:55 or 1:30, and show as m:ss after you leave the box.
  4. A question whose time falls outside its part is shown in red, right in that part.
  5. The embed code is hidden. “Watch this part” opens the clip so you can check the times.
  6. Click a part’s header to fold it away. Handy with 6–10 parts.
